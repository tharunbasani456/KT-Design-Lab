Small-Signal Modeling & Loop Design Guidelines:
• Capacitor ESR Zero: ωesr = 1/(C·Rc) introduces +90° phase lead at high frequency.
• Inductor ESR Rl & Cap ESR Rc: Increases damping (lowers Q) of the LC complex pole pair.
• RHP Zero (Boost / Buck-Boost): Limits closed-loop bandwidth fc < fRHP / 5.
• Phase Margin vs. Peak Overshoot: PM = 45° → 16% overshoot | PM = 60° → 4.6% overshoot (optimal) | PM = 30° → 37% overshoot.

Design provenance and modeling checklist

✓ Non-ideal CCM state-space matrices with Rl (DCR) and Rc (ESR)
✓ Architecture-based plant filtering (VMC, ACMC, and Cascaded Voltage+Current)
✓ Cascaded Vout/IL closed-loop response models the inner current loop as Ti(s)=ωci/(s+ωci) and includes ZOH + computation delay to evaluate Vout/Vref
✓ Op-Amp hardware realization with E96/E24 component sizing and vertical Zf(s)/Zin(s) transfer functions
✓ Phase Margin to transient overshoot mapping
✓ Selected-compensator pointer-driven C engine shared across 4 loops (Buck V/I & Boost V/I)

Engineering limits — read before using results

This platform implements small-signal state-space averaged CCM models including inductor ESR (Rl) and capacitor ESR (Rc), K-factor exact phase-margin synthesis, analog Op-Amp circuit synthesis, and pointer-driven 4-loop C-code generation for the selected compensator structure. Always verify switching ripple stability, slope compensation (if peak current mode), op-amp gain-bandwidth product (GBWP > 20× fp2), and hardware protections before powering prototypes.
